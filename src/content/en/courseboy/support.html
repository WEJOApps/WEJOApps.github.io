Frequently asked questions

How do I import a course?

Open a GPX or TCX file in CourseBoy from the Files app, iCloud Drive, AirDrop, a mail attachment, or another app's share button. You can also pick a file or folder directly from the app's home screen.

Tracking stops when I turn off the screen.

Check that iOS Settings › CourseBoy › Location is set to "While Using the App" or higher. With "Always", tracking and recording continue without the status bar indicator while the screen is off or you are using another app. In Low Power Mode, iOS may reduce background location updates.

I can't hear voice guidance.

Check that Settings › Course Tracking › Voice Guidance is on in the app, and check the device volume and silent mode. Voice guidance plays only while you are tracking a course.

Elevation differs from other apps.

While recording, the app uses both GPS altitude and the barometer to measure climbs and descents. Weather-driven pressure changes and GPS error mean values can differ slightly between devices and apps. If you don't allow Motion & Fitness access, the app can't use the barometer and elevation is less accurate.

Do my activities move to a new device?

Courses and activities are stored only on your device. Use a device backup and restore, or create a zip file with Settings › Activity Backup › Export All Activities and load it on the new device with Import Activities from Backup. Recorded activities are also available as FIT files in the Files app under "On My iPhone › CourseBoy › activity".

I want to turn off crash reports.

Turn off Settings › Problem Reporting › Send Crash Reports in the app. Crash reports never include your location or activity records.
